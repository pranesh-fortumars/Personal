# MASTER SPECIFICATION — AURELIA: THE INVITATION WORLD

## 1. Executive Summary
**AURELIA — THE INVITATION WORLD** is an ultra-luxury bespoke stationery and digital invitation atelier application. Tagline: *"Every Celebration Begins With An Invitation."*

The platform bridges heritage South Indian artistry, royal typography, and modern digital commerce with interactive 3D multi-page book viewing, instant multilingual switching (Tamil, English, Bilingual), live palette transformations, dynamic personalisation studios, cart persistence, and seamless direct WhatsApp order dispatch with complimentary delivery across India.

## 4. Key Functional Modules
- **Interactive 3D Multi-Page Booklet Viewer**: Physics-inspired realistic page-flip transitions, cover-to-RSVP pages, fullscreen immersion, and mobile touch swipe.
- **Real-Time Colorway Matrix**: Dynamic switching across 4+ palettes per design updating borders, typography, background gradients, and ornaments.
- **Multilingual Support**: Real Unicode Tamil, English, and synchronized Bilingual presentation without losing customer drafts.
- **Personalisation Studio**: Dynamic category-aware fields with real-time preview synchronisation, local storage draft preservation, and reset features.
- **Transparent Commercial Engine**: Strict unit pricing (₹5 – ₹2,000), quantity multiplication, 100% complimentary delivery nationwide, and configurable Grand Launch 50% promotions.
- **WhatsApp Direct Order Link Generator**: Encoded WhatsApp API integration dispatching formatted structured orders to `+918072854541`.
- **FastAPI / SQLAlchemy Backend & SQLite/PostgreSQL Database**: Complete REST API endpoints for templates, categories, variants, customizations, cart items, customer orders, and launch promotions.
- **Protected Administrator Dashboard**: Role-based administrative suite for inventory management, price adjustments, Tamil/English content editing, order lifecycle tracking, and promotional offer settings.
