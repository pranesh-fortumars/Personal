# USER MANUAL — AURELIA: THE INVITATION WORLD

Welcome to **AURELIA — THE INVITATION WORLD**.
*Tagline: Every Celebration Begins With An Invitation.*

This manual guides customers, designers, and atelier staff through the features and capabilities of the platform.

---

## 1. Exploring the Invitation World
1. **Homepage Exhibition**: Experience floating invitations, 3D wax seals, and layered paper textures with mouse parallax.
2. **Collection Browsing**: Access dedicated collections:
   - Weddings, Engagements, and Receptions
   - Tamil Heritage (Muhurtham)
   - Mehendi & Haldi Ceremonies
   - Birthdays (Kids & Milestone Adult)
   - Baby Showers & Seemantham / Valaikappu
   - Housewarming (Grihapravesam)
   - Anniversaries, Naming Ceremonies & Manjal Neerattu Vizha
3. **Interactive Search & Filtering**: Filter all 60 templates by category, price tier (₹5 to ₹2,000), colorway, and language.

---

## 2. Using the 3D Invitation Viewer
When you select an invitation design:
1. **Booklet Turning**: Click the **Next** or **Previous** buttons, click page thumbnails, or swipe left/right on mobile screens to turn pages in 3D perspective.
2. **Fullscreen Immersion**: Click the **Expand (Maximize)** icon to preview your invitation in distraction-free fullscreen view.
3. **Multi-Page Inspection**: Navigate through Cover, Primary Invitation, Event Details & Schedule, and RSVP / Closing pages.

---

## 3. Customising Your Invitation in Real Time
1. **Colourway Swatches**: Choose from coordinated palettes (e.g., *Emerald & Gold*, *Sapphire & Silver*, *Burgundy & Champagne*, *Ivory & Antique Gold*). The borders, typography, background gradients, and ornaments update instantly across every page.
2. **Language Selector**:
   - **தமிழ் (Tamil)**: Displays traditional Tamil phrases and Unicode calligraphy.
   - **ENGLISH**: Contemporary luxury editorial wording.
   - **BILINGUAL**: Harmonious dual-language arrangement.
3. **Personalisation Studio**: Fill in your custom details:
   - Host Name & Couple Names
   - Event Date & Auspicious Muhurtham Time
   - Venue & Address
   - RSVP Phone Number
   - Personal Message or Blessing
   Click **Save Draft** to store your entries locally, or **Reset** to return to defaults.

---

## 4. Pricing, Cart & Complimentary Delivery
1. **Quantity Selection**: Choose your required invitation quantity.
2. **Price Calculation**: Clearly displayed as:
   $$\text{Total} = \text{Unit Price} \times \text{Quantity}$$
3. **Complimentary Shipping**: Free delivery to your doorstep across India is guaranteed on every order.
4. **Cart Management**: Review your selections, edit quantities, or proceed to checkout directly from the cart drawer.
